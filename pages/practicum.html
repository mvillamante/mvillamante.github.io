Nature of Practicum

Internship Responsibilities

As a Business Analyst Intern at OGIS Philippines, I supported the development and enhancement of enterprise Power BI dashboards used for business reporting and decision-making. My work involved creating interactive visualizations, developing dynamic DAX calculations, validating data against source systems, and assisting in dashboard testing before deployment. These responsibilities strengthened my understanding of business intelligence, data modeling, report optimization, and collaborative software development within an enterprise setting.

Power BI Dashboard Development

Designed interactive dashboards, KPI cards, slicers, drill-through pages, and executive reports.

DAX Development

Developed calculated measures, dynamic KPIs, latest-value calculations, and business metrics using DAX.

Business Data Validation

Validated dashboard outputs against source spreadsheets and SharePoint data before deployment.

Report Navigation

Improved dashboard layouts, navigation, bookmarks, and user experience for business users.

Reflection

Overall Synthesis

My practicum at OGIS Philippines marked the transition from academic learning to real-world professional experience. Throughout 480 hours of training, I developed practical skills in creating Power BI dashboards, writing DAX measures, validating business data, and supporting enterprise reporting solutions. More importantly, I learned that successful business analytics goes beyond technical implementation—it requires understanding business requirements, maintaining data accuracy, and communicating insights in a way that enables informed decision-making.

The experience also taught me the importance of adaptability, continuous learning, and attention to detail. Working on actual client projects was both humbling and inspiring, as it challenged me to apply my knowledge while learning from experienced professionals. Collaborating with consultants, meeting project deadlines, and presenting solutions strengthened my confidence, professionalism, and ability to work effectively within a team.

Looking back, this practicum served as a foundation for my career in technology. It reinforced my passion for data analytics and business intelligence while preparing me to contribute meaningful, data-driven solutions in the workplace. The lessons, experiences, and values I gained throughout this journey will continue to guide my growth as I pursue opportunities in the IT industry.
